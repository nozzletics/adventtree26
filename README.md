# Advent Tree

A 3D-printable mechanical advent calendar in the shape of a Christmas tree, by **nozzletics**.

![Advent Tree](manual/f01.png)

24 little boxes hang on a tree of four tiers. A single drum runs through the centre of the tree, and a
knob under the cap turns it one click per day. Every box is held by its own latch and a pair of magnets:
only the box for the day the knob points at can be pulled out. The cap on top hides the knob and is
also a secret compartment; a two-part star sits on its tip.

- 4 tiers × 6 boxes = 24 compartments, plus the secret compartment in the cap
- about 275 mm across the bottom tier and about 45 cm tall without the star
- the largest part (the base) needs a 220 × 220 mm print bed
- three filament colours in the reference model: green, ivory and terra
- the tiers, top floor and cap are joined with bayonet locks, so there are no screws in the structure

## How it works

![Exploded view](manual/f02.png)

Each box has a window in its inner wall. A latch pivots on a filament pin in the tier's tube: its tooth
hangs in the box window, and its tail rests just above a flange on the drum. To pull a box out, the tooth
has to ride up its 45° face, so the tail has to go down. On every day but the right one the flange is in
the way and the box stays locked. On the box's own day the knob has turned the drum so that a notch sits
under the tail: the tail drops in and the box slides out. A magnet in the box and one in the tube pull it
back in and hold it snugly.

The drum is made of one segment per tier. Each segment couples to the one below through a collar with one
wide tooth, so it can only go in one way and the notches for all 24 days line up with their boxes. Click
springs in the top floor give the knob a detent at every day, and the dial on the top floor shows days
1–24.

## Variants

| Variant | Script | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Mech** (main model) | `3d_model/advent_tree_mech.py` | Drum, latches and magnets as described above. Covered by the assembly manual. |
| **Motor kit** | `3d_model/advent_tree_motor.py` | Add-on for the mech variant: replaces the knob with a 28BYJ-48 stepper (ULN2003 driver) and adds a mount for a 40 × 40 mm speaker. Not covered by the manual. Firmware: coming soon. |
| **Hook** | `3d_model/advent_tree_generator.py` | Earlier design: each box sits on a hidden tray and is held by a hook on the tier's post; any box can be opened at any time. |

## Working with the model

The model is generated by Python scripts in Blender 4.2 or newer (1 Blender unit = 1 mm).

1. Open `3d_model/AdventTree_nozzletics.blend`.
2. In the Text Editor, select the generator script and edit the parameter dictionary `P` at the top
   (number of boxes per tier, tier height, radius, overhang, tolerances and so on).
3. Press **Alt+P**. The whole tree is rebuilt.

To re-render the manual figures, run `man_render.py`, `man_figs.py`, `man_steps.py` and then
`man_steps2.py` to `man_steps4.py` inside Blender, in that order. The images are written to `manual/`.

Print a fit test first: one box (halves A and B) and its carrier.

## Assembly

See [`AdventTree_Assembly_Manual.pdf`](AdventTree_Assembly_Manual.pdf). In short:

1. Glue the tube magnets into the base and carriers, all with the same pole facing out.
2. Build the tree from the bottom up: drum segment, latches on filament pins, then the next carrier
   locked with its bayonet, until all four tiers are done.
3. Fit the top floor and the knob.
4. Load the boxes one day at a time: turn the knob to the day, then push that box in.
5. Put on the cap and the star.

**Hardware:** 48 disc magnets 6 × 3 mm, 1.75 mm filament for the pins (24 × 17.7 mm and 1 × 9.5 mm), and
optionally 24 self-tapping screws 2 × 6 mm as latch weights. You also need super glue, flush cutters and a
file.
